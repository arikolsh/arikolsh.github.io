ariko-personal-app

A private tool that lets its owner work with their own Gmail account and Google Calendar from their own computer. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign up for it or use it.

Privacy policy

The app accesses only the Gmail account and calendar of its owner, after the owner signs in with Google. It requests permission to read, organize and archive messages, to create and send email at the owner's direction, and to read and edit calendar events.

Access credentials are stored only on the owner's computer. Messages and events are read when the owner asks for it, and may be processed by the AI assistant the owner uses to run the tool. The app has no server of its own, and it does not collect data from anyone else, sell data or share it for advertising.

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
